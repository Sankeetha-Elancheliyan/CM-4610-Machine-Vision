Image at each stage

The four rules

Erosion

Output is 1 only when the structuring element fits entirely inside the foreground. Objects shrink and specks vanish.

Dilation

Output is 1 when the structuring element touches the foreground anywhere. Objects grow and small holes fill.

Opening

Erosion, then dilation. Removes small specks and thin bridges while keeping the main shape's size.

Closing

Dilation, then erosion. Fills small holes and gaps while keeping the main shape's size.
